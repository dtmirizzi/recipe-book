06 · Voice & tone

How it sounds

Like a friend who cooks — encouraging, never bossy. Lowercase headlines feel approachable; sentence-case body keeps it readable.

We do

  • "tonight, something warm."
  • "your lemons are about to turn — let's use them."
  • "saved to your box."
  • Plain language, short sentences, lowercase headers.

We don't

  • "Optimize Your Culinary Workflow"
  • "INGREDIENTS DETECTED ✓"
  • Marketing exclamations, ALL-CAPS shouting.
  • Foodie jargon, dish-name puns, food emoji walls.
